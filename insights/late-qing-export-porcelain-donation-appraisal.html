Why this appraisal question matters

Chinese export porcelain donation appraisal is not only a price question. It begins with object identity, use case, effective date, condition, provenance, and comparable market evidence.

Evidence to organize first

  • Photographs, measurements, medium, marks, inscriptions, and labels.
  • Acquisition records, prior appraisals, provenance notes, and condition evidence.
  • The appraisal purpose: donation, estate, insurance, sale planning, or family division.
  • Comparable sales that match Chinese export porcelain, not only broad visual similarity.

Cross-cultural valuation issue

The first comparable sales are too broad and do not match form, condition, period language, or sale venue.
