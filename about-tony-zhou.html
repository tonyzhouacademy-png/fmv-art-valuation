Chinese art history background

Beyond appraisal reporting, Tony's work is grounded in Chinese art history, close looking, judgment training, and the long-term formation of visual confidence. This art historical background supports appraisal work when object category, provenance, inscription, condition, and market language need careful translation.

His research interests include Sung and Yuan art, fourteenth-century cross-cultural artistic exchange, material culture, export mindscapes, and the limits of artistic translation. This background matters because Chinese and Asian art valuation often depends on translating category, provenance, inscription, condition, and market language across cultures.

How this connects to FMV appraisal

A fair market value file needs more than a number. It needs a defensible chain from object identity to market category, from condition to comparable sales, from provenance to value premise, and from cultural meaning to the actual market being used.

This is why the site is organized by problem: donation appraisal, estate valuation, insurance appraisal, and Chinese and Asian art valuation.
