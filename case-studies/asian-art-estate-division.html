Anonymized case study

Asian art estate division: making a mixed inherited collection administratively clear.

The family needed a practical estate valuation path before deciding what to retain, sell, donate, insure, or distribute.

The question

A mixed collection included Chinese ceramics, Japanese prints, small bronzes, textiles, and decorative objects. The family asked for "the value of the collection," but the real need was a valuation map.

The evidence gap

Some objects had cultural interest but limited market value. A smaller group deserved individual FMV work. A third group needed better identification before any reliable comparable sales could be selected.

The appraisal approach

  • Created a triage inventory with images, dimensions, object type, and confidence level.
  • Separated estate administration needs from future sale or insurance questions.
  • Identified objects requiring specialist market research.
  • Built a family-facing summary that explained why values differed across categories.

Outcome

The family received a clearer path: collection triage first, individual FMV files second, and separate insurance review only for objects retained after division.
