Late Qing Export Porcelain Donation Appraisal Case Study

SEO guide for Chinese export porcelain donation appraisal, fair market value evidence, Asian art valuation, and appraisal preparation.

The client context

A donor is preparing a charitable contribution and has older purchase records, auction screenshots, and incomplete condition notes.

The valuation problem

The first comparable sales are too broad and do not match form, condition, period language, or sale venue.

The appraisal approach

  • Rebuild the object description with category, dimensions, condition, and images.
  • Separate old purchase records and estimates from completed comparable sales.
  • Identify which market venue and value premise fit the assignment.
  • Link the case back to the appropriate service pathway.

Practical outcome

The project becomes a focused evidence file rather than a broad request for "what is it worth."
