Can one art appraisal serve donation, estate, and insurance?

Usually no. Donation, estate, and insurance assignments can require different value premises, effective dates, intended users, and documentation. Start with FMV basics, then choose the right service path.

What makes an art donation appraisal different?

Donation appraisal is tied to charitable contribution documentation and fair market value support. Review official IRS resources and read the donation appraisal pathway.

Can auction estimates replace an art appraisal?

No. Auction estimates can be useful clues, but a defensible appraisal needs object-specific analysis, effective date context, condition evidence, and relevant comparable sales. See the Chinese porcelain donation case.

Why is Chinese and Asian art valuation difficult?

It often requires translation across cultural taxonomy, market language, inscriptions, condition terms, attribution conventions, and regional sale venues. Read the Chinese and Asian art valuation guide.

What should I prepare before asking for an appraisal?

Prepare images, dimensions, medium, marks, labels, inscriptions, provenance, acquisition records, prior appraisals, condition notes, and the reason the value is needed.

What evidence matters for Late Qing Export Porcelain Donation Appraisal?

Object photographs, dimensions, condition, provenance, acquisition records, intended use, effective date, and comparable sales matter most. Read related FAQ.
